Report: Global AI-Powered Customer Support Market Overview
Date: Q1 2025
Source: MarketMind Research Index

Market Trends:
1. Transition from simple Decision-Tree Chatbots to Autonomous LLM Agents (e.g. Fin AI, Zendesk AI).
2. Outcome-based pricing models (charging per successful query resolution rather than per seat).
3. Demand for zero-hallucination guarantees and strict grounding in company documentation.
4. Omnichannel integration across WhatsApp, Email, and Web Widgets.

Market Growth:
- CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate): 23.5% (2024 to 2028).
- Estimated Market Size by 2028: $18.4 Billion.

Key Recommendations for Startups:
- Focus on niche vertical markets (e.g., Healthcare CS, E-commerce Logistics CS).
- Offer transparent pricing with hybrid seat + resolution tiers.
- Provide sub-second latency and zero-code setup.
